What we store

When you sign up, we store one record for your address:

  • your email address;
  • whether it is confirmed, your plan (Free or Pro), and the categories you chose;
  • the dates you signed up, were sent a confirmation, confirmed, were sent an “already subscribed” email, and asked for Pro;
  • a random code that makes the links in your emails work;
  • when you ask for a sign-in link: a one-way hash of that link’s code, when it was sent and when it expires (15 minutes later). It is cleared once you use the link.

We also count events, such as a sign-up, a sign-in email or an unsubscribe, with only the type, the time and the plan. These counts hold no address, so they stay after you leave. To limit automated sign-ups, we keep a shortened, one-way hash of the connection’s IP address (never the address itself) and delete it within a few hours.

Cookies

When you confirm your address or sign in, we set one cookie, tpr_session, so the site knows it’s you. It holds your record’s id (a one-way hash of your address, not the address itself), an expiry date and a signature. It lasts until you close the browser or sign out, and 12 hours at most. We set no advertising or tracking cookies.

What we use it for

Only to send the digest you asked for, to let you sign in and manage or end it, and to count sign-ups and unsubscribes. We don’t sell it, share it for advertising, or add you to any other list.

Who handles it for us
