Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

The account and the daily email are free. Pro is also free while it’s being tested.

Is there a password?

No. Your email address is your account. To sign in, ask for a link and open it from your inbox.

How do I stop the emails?

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes the account.

What do you store about me?

Your email address, the dates you signed up and confirmed, and whether you asked for Pro. Signing in sets one cookie on that browser so the site knows it’s you. It holds no address and lasts until you close the browser or sign out (12 hours at most).

Can I trust what it finds?

The items are gathered by an AI model with web search and are not verified. Check each source link before you act on one.
