How billing works

Nothing is charged while Pro is being tested. When payments open, this is how they’ll work:

  • You pay on your account page, after confirming your email. Payments are handled by Razorpay.
  • On your account page you choose which categories you get, how many emails a day, and how long each billing period is ({{PRO_PERIODS}}); the price depends on all three, and you see it before you pay.
  • Pro renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel. You approve the first payment; later payments are taken under that approval, and before each one you’re notified at least 24 hours ahead, as the RBI’s e-mandate rules require (for cards, by your bank).
  • Pro starts a minute or two after you pay, and your account page shows the next renewal date.
  • Cancel any time on your account page: two clicks, no reason asked. Pro stays on until the end of the billing period you’ve paid for, then the account is Free.
  • If a renewal payment fails, Pro stays on while Razorpay tries again. If it stops trying, the account goes back to Free and nothing more is charged.
